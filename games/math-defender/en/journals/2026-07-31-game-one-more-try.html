I Am Going to Try Making a Game

I am not a game developer.

My day job is software engineering.

I have experimented with Unity for years. Whenever an idea appeared, I built a prototype. Then another idea arrived, and I started another project.

Most of them ended there.

With a full-time job and children to raise, finishing a game turned out to be much harder than starting one.

This time feels a little different.

Because of AI.

Work that I once had to handle alone can now be shared with AI. I can discuss design, write code, organize documents, and get reviews.

AI is not making the game for me.

I still have to choose the direction and make the decisions.

But it now feels possible to keep going longer and more consistently than before.

So I am starting again.

The project is called Math Defender.

The goal is to release it on Steam and Google Play.

I am not expecting some enormous success.

The first goal is simpler: finish something and release it instead of stopping at another prototype.

I plan to record the good parts, the failed attempts, and the decisions along the way.

Months from now, I may read this and laugh—or discover that this became yet another prototype.

Still, this time I want to see it through.
